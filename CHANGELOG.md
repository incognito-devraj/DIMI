What's new in DIMI 1.0.3

A smoother, more dependable day-to-day experience.

• The Home screen now shows a cloud animation while your dashboard syncs.
• Reminder sounds behave more consistently, including when your phone is face down.
• Reminder and planner alerts now show the correct follow-up after an action.
• Editing a completed planner event makes it active again.
• Finance pages stay in place when switching tabs.
